Anatomy · Right Half

Target frame (dashed): the visible frame of the display, minus menu bar and Dock, split by the action.

Inset 6 pt on every side, so adjacent previews never touch and the wallpaper shows between them.

Fill accent at 18% (dark) / 14% (light) over a 6 pt backdrop blur. The window behind stays legible.

Stroke 1.5 pt accent at 92% / 88%, plus a 1 pt outer keyline (black 35% in dark, white 60% in light) so the edge holds on both bright sky and dark rock.

Radius 10 pt, matching macOS 14 window corners.

Timing fade in 120 ms ease-out after a 40 ms dwell; moves between targets in 90 ms ease-in-out; fades out 100 ms. Reduce Motion: no move animation, crossfade 80 ms.

Increase Contrast fill 28%, stroke 2 pt at 100%.
