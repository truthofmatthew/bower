Increase Contrast swaps the HUD material for an opaque fill (97%), outlines every wedge with a 1 pt stroke at 72% label colour, and draws glyph frames in the primary label colour instead of secondary.

Keyboard: ⌃⌥Space opens with focus on the wedge under the cursor. Arrow keys step through the inner ring, Tab moves to the outer ring, → opens a ▸ arc, Return runs, Esc cancels. Focus is a 2 pt accent ring plus an accent tint; hover is a solid accent fill, so the two never look alike.
