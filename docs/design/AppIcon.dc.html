Concept

The bowerbird builds an arched avenue of sticks and lays out collected objects in front of it with great care, often favouring blue. The icon is that arch over four arranged tiles: a desk of windows. One tile is satin blue for the bird; the rest use the Bower Teal family. No gloss, no glow; one soft top-to-bottom body tone as macOS icons have.
